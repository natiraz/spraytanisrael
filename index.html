The Protocol

Great color is a collaboration.

A few quiet rituals before and after your session decide how evenly your shade develops — and how gracefully it leaves.

Complete any waxing or shaving now, so pores can settle. Exfoliate gently the day before — polished skin takes color like fresh canvas takes paint. Then leave your skin alone: no oils, no self-tanner remnants, nothing that competes.

Arrive with bare skin — no moisturizer, deodorant, perfume, or makeup on the areas being tanned. Bring loose, dark clothing and open shoes for the journey home. The solution needs room to breathe; so do you.

This is when your shade develops — treat it like wet varnish. No water, no sweat, no tight straps. When the time is up, rinse in lukewarm water without soap and pat, never rub, yourself dry. What emerges is the color we composed together.

Moisturize morning and night — hydrated skin holds color longest. Keep showers short and lukewarm, and give pools and long sea swims a few days' grace. Cared for this way, your glow retires slowly, evenly, and entirely on its own terms.
